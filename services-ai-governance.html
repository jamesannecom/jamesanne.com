Build an AI governance framework

Define the principles, roles and review steps that guide how your organisation builds, buys and uses AI, then show customers and regulators where you stand.

Email me about this or book a call (opens in a new tab)
Format
Embedded engagement, on-site and remote
Duration
8 to 12 weeks
Participants
Product, engineering, data, legal, security, HR and communications leads
What you get
  • A framework your team can apply on a normal Tuesday
  • Named decision owners, a review process and an AI inventory
  • A public statement of your AI standards, including how you’ll tell customers when AI is involved

The program includes

  1. Map current and planned AI use across products, internal tools and vendors.

Assessment

EU AI Act readiness review

Find out which of your products the AI Act covers, whether you count as a provider or a deployer, and what to do next.

Email me about this or book a call (opens in a new tab)
Format
Document review and working sessions, remote
Duration
3 to 5 weeks
Participants
Leadership, product and engineering leads, and your legal counsel
What you get
  • An inventory of your AI systems, with a risk classification for each
  • Your obligations by role, with a timeline
  • A prioritised action plan to brief your lawyers and investors

The program includes

  1. Inventory your AI systems and the general-purpose models you build on.
  2. Classify each one: prohibited, high-risk, transparency obligations, or minimal risk.
  3. Clarify your role as provider, deployer or both, product by product.
  4. Find the gaps in documentation, transparency, human oversight and AI literacy.
  5. Prioritise actions and brief your counsel. This is a governance review, not legal advice: it works alongside your lawyers.
