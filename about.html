Anne James

Anne James is a cultural anthropologist and Fractional CMO who advises startups and AI teams across Europe and the US on growth strategy and AI governance. Certified as an AI Governance Professional by the International Association of Privacy Professionals (IAPP), she is a member of the Center for AI and Digital Policy’s AI Policy Clinic.

She has spent more than a decade as Head of Marketing for enterprise software companies, including AI companies partnered with NVIDIA and Anthropic. She knows how AI is built, sold and bought, and what the sales deck leaves out.

Her training as an anthropologist shapes how she works: she runs growth as a research programme, with each experiment designed around the evidence from the one before.

Outside tech, she founded and published Zymbol, an internationally distributed print magazine of contemporary literature and art, from 2012 until its sale to a larger publisher in 2017.

She is based in Lisbon, Portugal, and welcomes invitations to speak at events worldwide.

Anthropology taught me to look past what an organisation says to how it behaves. Plenty of earnest AI principles have been undone by a launch decision nobody owned. Usually no one notices until a customer or a regulator does. I won’t let you sleepwalk into a reputational crisis.
