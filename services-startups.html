Research

Customer and market insight

Find out why customers buy, why deals stall and why accounts leave, before you rewrite your positioning or raise your next round.

Email me about this or book a call (opens in a new tab)
Format
Customer and prospect interviews, a win/loss review and a short survey
Duration
4 to 8 weeks
Participants
Customers, lost prospects and churned accounts, plus your sales and customer success teams
What you get
  • A findings report in the language your buyers actually use
  • An ideal customer profile and buying-committee map, backed by evidence
  • Messaging, product and pricing recommendations tied to what you heard

The program includes

  1. Set the questions: what you need to decide, and which customers and segments to hear from.
  2. Recruit and interview buyers, users, lost deals and churned accounts, with clear consent and careful data handling.

Policy and standards

Enterprise-ready AI governance

Enterprise buyers now ask how you build, test and govern your AI. Have good answers ready, and make them part of how you sell.

Email me about this or book a call (opens in a new tab)
Format
Embedded engagement, remote
Duration
6 to 10 weeks
Participants
Founders, and your product, engineering, security and sales leads
What you get
  • An AI governance policy, roles and review process sized for a small team
  • Ready answers for the AI sections of security questionnaires and procurement reviews
  • A public trust page on how your AI works, what data it uses, and its limits

The program includes

  1. Map your AI: models, data sources, vendors, and where AI shows up in your product.

  4. Prepare for sales: questionnaire answers, a trust page and a short briefing for your sales team.
  5. Set up a lightweight review for new features, models and vendors.

Assessment

EU AI Act readiness review

Find out which of your products the AI Act covers, whether you count as a provider or a deployer, and what to do next.

Email me about this or book a call (opens in a new tab)
Format
Document review and working sessions, remote
Duration
3 to 5 weeks
Participants
Founders, product and engineering leads, and your legal counsel
What you get
  • An inventory of your AI systems, with a risk classification for each
  • Your obligations by role, with a timeline
  • A prioritised action plan to brief your lawyers and investors

The program includes

  1. Inventory your AI systems and the general-purpose models you build on.
  2. Classify each one: prohibited, high-risk, transparency obligations, or minimal risk.
  3. Clarify your role as provider, deployer or both, product by product.
  4. Find the gaps in documentation, transparency, human oversight and AI literacy.
  5. Prioritise actions and brief your counsel. This is a governance review, not legal advice: it works alongside your lawyers.

“Do you have an AI policy?”

It’s on enterprise security questionnaires now, and a vague answer slows deals down. I can help you reach a position you can explain and defend.
