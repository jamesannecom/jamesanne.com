Research

Strategic insight research

Before a major bet, find out how your customers and your own teams really work, and where a new product, market or AI tool will fit or fail.

Email me about this or book a call (opens in a new tab)
Format
Interviews, observation and short surveys, on-site and remote
Duration
6 to 12 weeks
Participants
Customers, frontline and operations teams, and leadership sponsors, depending on the question
What you get
  • A findings report with clear implications for the decision in front of you
  • Customer or employee journey maps based on observed behaviour
  • Baseline measures you can track after launch

The program includes

  1. Frame the decision and the questions whose answers would change it.
  2. Design the research: who to hear from, where to observe, and how personal data is protected.
  3. Listen and observe: interviews, and time alongside people where the work actually happens.
  4. Analyse and benchmark: code and count what you heard, compare groups, and set it against market data.
  5. Turn findings into recommendations, present them to leadership, and close the loop with the people who took part.

Format
Facilitated engagement, on-site and remote
Duration
8 to 12 weeks
Participants
Executive sponsors, and product, data, risk and business-unit leads
What you get

Policy and standards

Build an AI governance framework

Define the principles, roles and review steps that guide how your organisation builds, buys and uses AI, then show customers and regulators where you stand.

Email me about this or book a call (opens in a new tab)
Format
Embedded engagement, on-site and remote
Duration
8 to 12 weeks
Participants
Product, engineering, data, legal, security, HR and communications leads
What you get
  • A framework your team can apply on a normal Tuesday
  • Named decision owners, a review process and an AI inventory
  • A public statement of your AI standards, including how you’ll tell customers when AI is involved

The program includes

  1. Map current and planned AI use across products, internal tools and vendors.
  2. Assess the risks to customers, employees, data protection and your contracts, mapped to the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001 and the NIST AI RMF.
  3. Draft principles and decision rules with your leadership team, including who signs off and when.
  4. Publish your standards: turn your key decisions into a plain-language statement for customers, partners and regulators.
  5. Set up a lightweight review process for new models, features and vendors, and hand over the framework with a plan to revisit it as regulation and practice change.

Workshop

AI vendor procurement workshop

Learn what to ask before you buy or renew an AI-enabled tool, and how to read the answers.

Email me about this or book a call (opens in a new tab)
Format
Facilitated workshop with your procurement, legal, security and IT teams
Duration
Half day
Participants
Procurement, legal, security, IT and the teams requesting tools (max. 12 people)
What you get
  • A reusable vendor question checklist
  • A simple approach to comparing vendors
  • A short decision record template

The program includes

  1. Frame the decision: what problem the tool should solve, and what could go wrong.
  2. Walk through the vendor questions: data use, training on your data, security, hosting and exit terms.
  3. Read real vendor claims and terms together and spot the gaps.
  4. Score a live example from your own shortlist.
  5. Agree on your review steps and who signs off.

Go-to-market

Launch an AI product without overclaiming

Position a new AI product so it sells, and make sure every claim in the launch stands up to customers, regulators and your own legal team.

Email me about this or book a call (opens in a new tab)
Format
Working sessions alongside your product marketing team
Duration
6 to 10 weeks
Participants
Product, product marketing, sales, legal and communications leads
What you get
  • Positioning, messaging and a launch plan grounded in customer evidence
  • A claims review: what you can say about accuracy, safety and data use, and how to back it up
  • Customer-facing disclosures and FAQs on how the AI works and where its limits are

The program includes

  1. Test the value with target customers before the launch copy is written.
  2. Set positioning and messaging with product and sales.

  4. Plan the launch: channels, sales enablement and customer communications.
  5. Measure early results and adjust the story.
