Embedded leadership

Fractional CMO

Founding marketer work on an ongoing basis: senior oversight of your whole marketing function, for startups that need a CMO’s judgement before they need a full-time one. If you’d rather test every channel against every persona because you’re not ready to commit, hire someone else.

Email me about this or book a call (opens in a new tab)
Format
Part of your leadership team two or more days a week, remote with on-site days as needed
Duration
Month to month
Participants
Founders, your board, sales and product leads, and the freelancers or agencies I bring in and manage
What you get
  • A growth strategy that names the buyers and channels you’re betting on, and why
  • A working marketing engine: CRM, attribution, website, content and campaigns
  • Pipeline forecasts your board can rely on, and unit economics your investors can check for themselves

The program includes

  1. Audit your positioning, your category, and the economic headwinds and tailwinds you’re selling into.
  2. Choose the bets: two or three buyer personas or industries to win, and the channels worth funding.
  3. Build the engine, with results measured from the first month.
  4. Run it week to week, bringing in freelancers or agencies where that’s cheaper than hiring.
  5. Report pipeline, conversion and acquisition cost to founders and the board, and cut what isn’t working.
  6. Hand over when you’re ready for a full-time head of marketing: I write the brief and help you hire.

Research and strategy

Insight and go-to-market strategy

What to know about your buyers’ world, and what to do with it. You get a snapshot report on the economic and cultural forces shaping how your buyers decide, and a go-to-market strategy built on what it finds.

Email me about this or book a call (opens in a new tab)
Format
Interviews, desk research and a data review, then working sessions on the strategy, remote
Duration
2 to 3 weeks, with updates whenever you need them
Participants
Founders, sales and product leads, and a sample of customers, prospects and lost deals
What you get
  • An insight report on the economic and cultural factors in your buyers’ context, written in the language your buyers actually use
  • A go-to-market strategy document: the segments to target first, positioning and messaging, and the channels worth backing
  • Updates when the market moves or you enter a new one

The program includes

  1. Set the questions: what you need to decide, and which buyers to hear from.
  2. Read the evidence you already have: CRM notes, call recordings, support tickets and reviews.
  3. Go into the field: interviews with customers, prospects and lost deals, alongside research into your category and the economic conditions your buyers face.
  4. Write the report: what’s shifting in your buyers’ world, and why it matters to you.
  5. Build the strategy from it, and walk your leadership team through both.

Go-to-market

Launch an AI product without overclaiming

Position a new AI product so it sells, and make sure every claim in the launch stands up to customers, regulators and your own legal team.

Email me about this or book a call (opens in a new tab)
Format
Working sessions alongside your product marketing team
Duration
6 to 10 weeks
Participants
Product, product marketing, sales, legal and communications leads
What you get
  • Positioning, messaging and a launch plan grounded in customer evidence
  • A claims review: what you can say about accuracy, safety and data use, and how to back it up
  • Customer-facing disclosures and FAQs on how the AI works and where its limits are

The program includes

  1. Test the value with target customers before the launch copy is written.
  2. Set positioning and messaging with product and sales.
  3. Review every claim against the evidence, the risk of “AI-washing”, and transparency rules such as the EU AI Act’s.
  4. Plan the launch: channels, sales enablement and customer communications.
  5. Measure early results and adjust the story.

It’s the question behind most board meetings, and another channel rarely answers it. I’ll find where growth is really stuck and build a plan your team can run.
