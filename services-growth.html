Research

Customer and market insight

Find out why customers buy, why deals stall and why accounts leave, before you rewrite your positioning, enter a new market or raise your next round.

Email me about this or book a call (opens in a new tab)
Format
Customer and prospect interviews, a win/loss review and a short survey
Duration
4 to 8 weeks
Participants
Customers, lost prospects and churned accounts, plus your sales and customer success teams
What you get
  • A findings report in the language your buyers actually use
  • An ideal customer profile and buying-committee map, backed by evidence
  • Messaging, product and pricing recommendations tied to what you heard

The program includes

  1. Set the questions: what you need to decide, and which customers and segments to hear from.
  2. Recruit and interview buyers, users, lost deals and churned accounts, with clear consent and careful data handling.
  3. Read the evidence you already have: CRM notes, call recordings, support tickets and reviews.
  4. Analyse: code what you heard, compare segments, and separate what people say from what they do.
  5. Turn findings into decisions on positioning, product and pricing, and share them with your team.

Positioning and go-to-market strategy

Decide who you’re for, what you’ll be known for, and how you’ll reach the buyers who matter most.

Email me about this or book a call (opens in a new tab)
Format
Facilitated working sessions, remote or on-site
Duration
6 to 10 weeks
Participants
Founders or leadership, and your product and sales leads
What you get
  • A positioning and messaging framework your whole team can use
  • A go-to-market plan: target segments, channels, pricing signals and sales motion
  • Core copy for your website and sales deck

Planning sprint

30/60/90-day marketing plan

A concrete plan for your next quarter of marketing, for founders doing it themselves or a new marketing hire who needs to start fast.

Email me about this or book a call (opens in a new tab)
Format
Interviews, an audit and two working sessions, remote
Duration
2 to 3 weeks
Participants
Founders or leadership, and whoever will run marketing
What you get
  • A 30/60/90-day plan with priorities, owners and targets
  • Quick wins you can ship in the first month
  • A short list of the metrics that matter at your stage, and how to track them

The program includes

  1. Interview founders, sales and product on goals, customers and constraints.
  2. Audit your website, content, channels, tools and data.

  5. Check in at day 30 on a follow-up call to adjust.

Go-to-market

Launch an AI product without overclaiming

Position a new AI product so it sells, and make sure every claim in the launch stands up to customers, regulators and your own legal team.

Email me about this or book a call (opens in a new tab)
Format
Working sessions alongside your product marketing team
Duration
6 to 10 weeks
Participants
Product, product marketing, sales, legal and communications leads
What you get
  • Positioning, messaging and a launch plan grounded in customer evidence
  • A claims review: what you can say about accuracy, safety and data use, and how to back it up
  • Customer-facing disclosures and FAQs on how the AI works and where its limits are

The program includes

  1. Test the value with target customers before the launch copy is written.
  2. Set positioning and messaging with product and sales.
  3. Review every claim against the evidence, the risk of “AI-washing”, and transparency rules such as the EU AI Act’s.
  4. Plan the launch: channels, sales enablement and customer communications.
  5. Measure early results and adjust the story.
